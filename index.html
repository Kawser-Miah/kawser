About

I'm Kawser Miah, a Flutter developer who builds production-grade Android & iOS apps that are fast, scalable, and built to last. Most recently, I built a real-time, WebSocket-powered marketplace app with live bidding and GPS tracking serving both customers and drivers. I specialize in Clean Architecture with Bloc and Riverpod state management, flexible screen-adaptive UI with flutter_screenutil, and CI/CD pipelines on GitHub Actions so every push stays analyzed, tested, and release-ready.

My work goes beyond standard CRUD apps, real-time messaging with presence and typing indicators, geolocation-based matching built with geohash search, and offline-first data layers that keep working without a connection. Beyond mobile, I build Python backends with FastAPI and integrate Machine Learning, from CNN-based image classification and TensorFlow inference to RAG pipelines, bringing intelligent features directly into my apps.

Currently building real-time marketplace products as a Trainee Flutter Developer at Join Venture AI, and pursuing a B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering at Green University of Bangladesh. I'm also a Top 50 finalist of 242 teams at HackTheAI 2025. I care about clean code, smooth UX, and shipping things that actually hold up in production.
